GNLY in the uterus: the marks below the line at the exon 3 and exon 4 edges are exon junction reads. Two of the 84 GNLY sequences cross the exon 3 to exon 4 junction (chr2:85,696,056 to 85,697,506), each at a different point. This is the normal (canonical) junction of the main transcript GNLY-201 (MANE Select), also used by 3 other protein-coding isoforms (GNLY-211, -224, -226); it is not alternative or new splicing. The sequences fall below the line only because the aligner soft-clips the few bases across the junction. The other imperfect GNLY sequences contain the known SNPs rs11127 and rs12845.

updated 2026-10-26 19:12 PST3.4 · selected genes vs gene expression

Uterus NK cells: the model's most important genes are not simply the most expressed genes.

Human uterus atlas, natural killer (NK) cells against the other 29 cell types. Each dot is one gene the model chose sequences on.

Spearman -0.026 between gene importance and gene expression (317 weighted genes): importance does not follow expression. The weighted genes are well expressed (median percentile 98.93; 83% of the importance is on the top 5% most expressed genes), but among them the order is unrelated to expression. GNLY, a cytotoxic NK gene, makes up most of the importance (also Fig 7, section 3.3).

How it is counted. Model: hca-uterus-mc5, all sequences, one model over 30 uterus cell types (macro AUC 0.906); p2single run of Sept 2026, before v0.12. Importance of a gene = |sum over its selected sequences of f × NK coefficient|; colour = sign (higher or lower score for NK than for the other cell types, which is not the same as higher expression in NK). 206 genes score higher for NK and 170 lower; 838 grey genes are chosen only through an anchor-present term, which has no gene, so importance 0. Expression: the authors' CELLxGENE counts (PremenopausalUterusDynamics, five healthy donors), counts per 10,000 per cell, mean over the same 40,436 cells (1,041 NK, 39,395 other); rank among 31,450 expressed genes. 59 weighted genes are not in the authors' matrix (mostly pseudogenes and mitochondrial rRNA) and are not shown.

Method

input
sequencing reads, with no reference genome needed to build the model
features
short sequences called directly on the reads, counted per cell
model
a multinomial elastic net (a linear model) that picks the features separating the cell types
genes
added afterwards, only as labels for sequences the model already chose
transfer
a feature counts in another dataset only if that dataset contains the identical sequence; no re-fitting, no re-alignment
score
probabilities over the cell types both datasets share, one-vs-rest AUC per cell type, averaged
