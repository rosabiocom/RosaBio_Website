Variant calling · single cell

Cancer mutations found in individual cells

THP-1 leukaemia cells (SRP268462) and non-small cell lung cancer (GSE316782)
  • p2single keeps exact sequences, so it detects variants in individual cells.
  • In THP-1 cells (Papalexi et al. 2021), p2single detected the known NRAS G12D variant (c.35G>A, p.Gly12Asp).
  • In a single-cell study of non-small cell lung cancer (Xi et al. 2026), p2single detected the cancer-driving KRAS G12V mutation (c.35G>T, p.Gly12Val) in 33 cells from one patient. All mutant cells with a known cell type were tumour epithelial cells, and no other patient had it.
  • A 5′ UTR variant in JAK2 was also found in more than 1,000 THP-1 cells (1,968), along with variants in many more genes, including COSMIC variants and ClinVar variants of unknown significance: a path to predicting their effect.

Definitions
  • AUC: how well a model separates two groups of cells (1 = perfect, 0.5 = chance). For many cell types, the average over types.
  • Correlation (r): agreement between predicted and measured levels (1 = perfect, 0 = none).
  • Macro F1: the average over cell types of a score that combines correct calls and missed cells (1 = perfect).
  • ROC curve: share of exhausted cells found against share of active cells wrongly called exhausted, as the cut-off moves; the area under it is the AUC.
  • Cosine similarity: agreement between the predicted and measured change in gene levels (1 = perfect).
  • Machine learning model: a sparse linear model on sequences read from each cell. Deep learning model: a neural network trained on the same sequences from 23 single-cell studies, then adapted to each task.
