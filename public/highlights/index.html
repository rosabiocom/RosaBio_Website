Definitions
  • AUC: how well a model separates two groups of cells (1 = perfect, 0.5 = chance). For many cell types, the average over types.
  • Correlation (r): agreement between predicted and measured levels (1 = perfect, 0 = none).
  • Macro F1: the average over cell types of a score that combines correct calls and missed cells (1 = perfect).
  • ROC curve: share of exhausted cells found against share of active cells wrongly called exhausted, as the cut-off moves; the area under it is the AUC.
  • Cosine similarity: agreement between the predicted and measured change in gene levels (1 = perfect).
  • Machine learning model: a sparse linear model on sequences read from each cell. Deep learning model: a neural network trained on the same sequences from 23 single-cell studies, then adapted to each task.
